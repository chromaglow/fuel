fuel · restart & reboot guide

Rebooting is safe. Here's how to get your gauge back.

Turn your PC off and on all you want — nothing here breaks. When you're back, it's three easy steps to see fuel glowing again.

▸
The 30-second version: after your PC restarts, open fuel's little window, glance at it to be sure it's awake, then use Claude Code like normal. From now on Claude quietly hands its boring jobs to your graphics card — and the gauge fills up.

After you reboot — do these in order

3 steps

Open the little window

Open PowerShell (tap the Start button, type powershell, press Enter) and paste this. The small see-through panel pops onto your desktop.

> cd "C:\dev\GitHub\fuel"; npx electron .

Don't see it? Press Ctrl+Alt+F — that shows or hides it.

Check that it's awake

Look at the bottom line of the panel. If you see real numbers like 2.1/16 GB · 40 W · 39°C, the sensors are alive and everything's good. 🎉

If the ring is grey and says OFFLINE, your GPU brain (Ollama) just hasn't woken up yet — see the fixes below.

Just use Claude Code like normal

That's it — no setup. The next time Claude does something mechanical (reformatting, repetitive edits), it sends the job to your graphics card instead of spending Claude. The ring lights up and the ≈ $ starts ticking.

It may sit at $0.00 for a while — that's normal. It only counts new work, and the reboot gives Claude a fresh start automatically.

Is everything OK? Tick these

  • ✓
    The panel is on screen. You ran the command in Step 1 (or pressed Ctrl+Alt+F).
  • ✓
    The bottom line shows live GPU numbers. GB, watts, and °C are moving — sensors are working.
  • ✓
    It's not stuck on OFFLINE. If it is, open the Ollama app and wait a few seconds.
  • ✓
    Claude is still wired up. Run the check below — you want it to say fully wired in green.
> cd "C:\dev\GitHub\fuel"; node integrations/install.mjs status

Everyday controls

hold Ctrl+Alt
CtrlAltF Show / hide the panel
CtrlAltI Grab & move it — press again to lock
CtrlAltQ Quit fuel completely
tray Right-click the tray icon (bottom-right of your screen) for the full menu

When something looks off

I can't find the window

hidden

Press Ctrl+Alt+F. Still nothing? Right-click the tray icon → Show HUD. It may have opened on another monitor — tray → Move to display.

The ring is grey / says OFFLINE

offline

Your GPU brain (Ollama) isn't running yet. Open the Ollama app from the Start menu and wait a few seconds — the ring comes back to life on its own.

It still says $0.00

normal

Not broken! It only counts new work sent to your GPU. It fills the first time Claude does a mechanical job. Give it time, or ask Claude to do some repetitive edits.

It's in the way / I can't click it

click-thru

On purpose — your clicks pass right through it. Press Ctrl+Alt+I to grab and drag it somewhere out of the way, then press it again to lock it back down.

The numbers look frozen

stuck

Close it with Ctrl+Alt+Q and start it again (Step 1). If the whole PC feels weird, a full reboot fixes it — and fuel is happy to be restarted.

I hate typing the command each time

tip

Right-click the tray icon → tick Launch at login. fuel will start by itself every time you turn the PC on — no command needed ever again.

Your setup is untouchedThe advanced "Valve" is switched off, so rebooting changes nothing about how Ollama runs. You don't need to fix anything there.

$0 each morning is normalThe dollar figure resets at midnight and yesterday isn't lost — it's just showing today's gauge.
